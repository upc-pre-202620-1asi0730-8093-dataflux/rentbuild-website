RentBuild: Simplifying Equipment Rental and Management

RentBuild was created to simplify and centralize equipment rental operations. The platform helps businesses manage equipment availability, reservations, rentals, deliveries, returns, incidents, and maintenance from one place.

  • Equipment Management: Register, organize, and monitor the status, location, and availability of each piece of equipment.
  • Rental Lifecycle Tracking: Manage reservations, rentals, deliveries, returns, inspections, and maintenance in a connected workflow.
  • Maintenance Control: Track incidents, inspections, repairs, and maintenance activities to keep equipment ready for future rentals.
